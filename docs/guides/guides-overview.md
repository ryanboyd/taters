The Guides are the "what is this and why would I use it" layer of the
documentation. Each page walks through a family of methods — text, audio,
video, pipelines — in plain English: what each method measures, when it is
the right tool, and where the ideas come from, with pointers into the
research literature so you can read further (and cite properly).

Those pages get you as far as a folder full of measures.
[Running Analyses](running-analyses.md) is the one that picks up from there:
which statistical question fits the data you have, what each answer actually
tells you, and what to do with it once you have it.

Taters also arrives with a bunch of ready-made dictionaries and archetypes.
[Built-in dictionaries and archetypes](bundled-dictionaries.md) lists every
one of them, what it measures, and the citation to use when it ends up in
something you publish.

What you will *not* find here is an exhaustive list of every parameter. That
lives in the [API reference](../api/api-overview.md), one click away, and it
stays there so these pages can stay readable.

If you would rather not write code at all, start with
[the Taters app](wizard.md): running `taters` in your terminal walks you
through the same choices these guides explain and builds the whole analysis
from your answers.
